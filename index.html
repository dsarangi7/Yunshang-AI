FAQ

Questions importers ask us

Straight answers on ordering, customization and shipping.

What is the minimum order quantity, and can I order samples first?

MOQ depends on the model and on how much customization you need — a standard catalogue model and a fully branded OEM run are quoted differently. Tell us the models and quantities you have in mind and we will confirm the MOQ in writing. You can request samples through the quote form to check fit, finish and features before you commit to volume.

How does your OEM/ODM process work, and what branding options do you offer?

OEM puts your brand on one of our existing models; ODM adapts or develops a product to your requirements. Every project follows five steps: inquiry, OEM design, production, quality control and worldwide shipping. Branding can cover the product, its packaging and accompanying materials, and we agree every detail with you before production starts.

Can I customize the lenses and add my own logo?

Yes. Our range already covers blue-light, sunglass, photochromic, polarized, iridescent, coated and clear lenses, and the W640 is compatible with myopia (prescription) frames. Lens options and logo placement are confirmed per model during the OEM design stage, with a sample for your approval.

What are your lead times and production capacity?

Lead time depends on the model, the quantity and the level of customization, so we quote it per order instead of publishing a generic figure. Standard catalogue models move faster than customized runs. Share your target delivery date in your inquiry and we will confirm a schedule before you place the order.

Which shipping options do you offer, and do you handle export documents?

We ship worldwide from Shenzhen and take care of export handling for every consignment. The shipping method — for example express courier, air freight or sea freight — is chosen with you based on volume, budget and timeline. Tell us your destination country and we will confirm the documents your import requires.

What payment terms do you accept?

Payment terms are agreed per order and confirmed in writing on your proforma invoice before production begins. They can depend on order size and on whether the order is customized. Ask in your inquiry and we will set out the options available to you.

What warranty and after-sales support do you provide?

Warranty terms are confirmed per model and per order, and written into your sales agreement. Every order passes our quality inspection before it ships. If an issue arises after delivery, contact us by email or WhatsApp and our team will work with you on a resolution.

Which languages do the AI features support?

Language coverage varies by model. The M10A offers AI translation in 139 languages, the AIMB-G2 Pro provides real-time voice and video translation, and the W640 adds dialogue translation, simultaneous interpretation and meeting minutes. Ask us for the current language list of any model you plan to import.

How do the glasses connect — is there a companion app?

The glasses pair with a smartphone over Bluetooth for audio, calls and the AI assistant. Models such as the W611 Pro, W630 and M10A also support Wi-Fi transfer of photos and videos to the companion app. We confirm app and phone compatibility for each model before you order.

How can I become a regional distributor?

Send us a quote request describing your company, your market and the models you want to carry. We will discuss volumes, branding and support for your region. Distributors buy factory-direct at our best rate, with OEM/ODM options to build their own line.
